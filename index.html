A small toolbox. Lots of possibilities.

Freeform brings open AI models from Hugging Face to your own device. Write and chat with SmolLM2 or Qwen, ask questions about readable PDF, DOCX, Markdown and text files, create images with FLUX.2 Klein, transcribe speech with Whisper, read English text with SpeechT5, and remove portrait backgrounds with MODNet.

Will it work on my laptop or phone?

The smallest text model and audio tools run on your CPU. Larger text models and images require a working WebGPU graphics adapter. An updated Chrome or Edge is a good starting point. Phones and older devices may run out of memory; smaller models help.

Is it really free and private?

There are no accounts, ads, trackers, or paid AI calls. Your device supplies the computation. You still use electricity and download data. Cloudflare serves the website; Hugging Face and its download infrastructure serve model files and can see ordinary download connection information, including your IP address.

Can I use any Hugging Face model?

This catalog includes models with compatible browser runtimes. Most Hugging Face repositories cannot run directly in a browser. Model sizes and relative capability labels are estimates, not quality benchmarks or promises that a device will fit them.

Your words stay with you.

Prompts, documents, images, and audio are processed in your browser. There is no backend inference, account system, analytics, advertising, or chat-history upload.

The website is delivered by Cloudflare. Model files come from Hugging Face, its download hosts, and MLC's compiled model library on GitHub. These providers receive ordinary network connection information. Fonts and runtime files are served with this site.

Models are stored in browser caches and, for images, browser-private storage. Conversations and uploaded files are kept in memory and disappear on reload. Download or copy results you want to keep. Do not use a shared device for private files.

AI output can be inaccurate. Document answers use matching excerpts, not a full reading of every page. Image generation is experimental and can be slow. Model licenses apply to each model separately from this website's MIT license.
